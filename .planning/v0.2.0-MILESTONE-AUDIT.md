## Cross-Phase Integration & E2E

The three phases form a single vertical: build (P1) → load+run (P2) → automated
gate (P3). End-to-end integration is directly exercised by CI — the gate consumes
the artifact P1 produces, loads it (P2), and runs the suite (P3). A green
`wasm-runtime-test` job is the integration proof; no wiring gaps.

## Notable finding (resolved during the milestone)

A suspected `ols_fit_predict_by` `MIN(x1)` discrepancy was investigated and traced
to a **duckdb-wasm Arrow-JS DECIMAL rendering quirk** (unscaled integer) in the
harness — not an extension bug. Fixed by formatting results through DuckDB
`::VARCHAR`. Full suite then 2095/2095.
